# Model card: ChurnCast 1.0.0

> Generated by `scripts/model_card.py` from `metrics.json` and `model.json`; do not edit by hand.

## Model details
- **Model:** logistic regression, all 15 features (experiment E2), unpenalised, maximum likelihood.
- **Artefact:** `model.json` schema 1.0, model version 1.0.0, trained by the python track (1.0.0).
- **Target:** `churned_next_month`: a subscriber with a paid invoice in month M has none in month M+1.
- **Training data:** export 2026-05 (contract 1.0.0), snapshots 2025-09 to 2026-02: 11,862 customer-months, churn rate 9.7 %.
- **Test data (out of time):** snapshots 2026-03, 2026-04: 5,148 customer-months, churn rate 8.8 %.

## Intended use
- Rank this month's active subscribers by risk so the retention team calls the top 10 % (its capacity) first, with the top reasons for each call.
- Not for: pricing an individual customer, refusing service, credit decisions, or any automated action without a person in the loop. Not validated outside TiffinBox's cities and plans.

### What the numbers mean for the retention team
Calling the top 10 % each month (516 calls over the two test months) reaches 137 of the customers who then cancelled: precision 26.6 %, recall 30.2 %, 3.0x better than calling at random. With the contract's assumptions (₹200 per call, 25 % of reached churners stay, 3 months of revenue each) that is ₹109,786 a month, against ₹63,001 for today's rule of thumb. The save rate is an assumption until a holdout experiment measures it.

## Limitations and ethical considerations
- Synthetic data: the case study's TiffinBox is fictional; the patterns are realistic, the people are not.
- Unseen drivers: acquisition channel and age band affect churn but are not features (channel has gaps before March 2025; age band is protected), so some slices are under- or over-predicted.
- Drift: a price change, a new channel or a competitor changes who churns. Monitor feature drift and calibration monthly and retrain through the champion/challenger gate (Phase 7).
- Explanations are contributions to a linear score, not reasons a customer gave. The optional AI explainer rewrites them in plain language and is checked by guardrails; it never sees the customer id.
